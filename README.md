# MoneyMan
MoneyMan is an application that helps you manage your money and expenses across different accounts. Just give the money man your money and he'll handle it securely.

## Features

### 1. Fully Local:
You don't have to worry where your personal financing information goes. Everything is stored on your device and the application runs offline.

### 2. Support for Multiple Accounts:
Don't like to keep your money in one place? That's perfectly fine. With MoneyMan you can create different accounts according to your needs.

### 3. Support for Different Currencies and Conversion Rates
Not every country deals with one currency only. The MoneyMan will add Euros to your Dollars account if you want him to. 

But what about the conversion rate? Well, all you have to do is tell him once and he'll remember the conversion rate from one currency to another. And of course, you can change the conversion rate anytime you want.

### 4. Different Ways to Interact with MoneyMan
Maybe you like to see stuff in a nice website, or maybe you prefer to just see text (no judging), or you are one of those guys that like to do everything from the command line (if you don't know what that is, bless your heart). In any case, MoneyMan can do it all!
